# Round 3 — prespecified two-phase protocol

Phase A: acquire and construct all samples, document provenance, freeze texts.json by timestamp and SHA-256 in manifest.json, then submit every frozen text to ZeroGPT. Do not call any endpoint on port 8323, including health. No detector code/model/service edits, commits or training.

Target 160 samples: human native/proficient proxy 40; human non-native 40; AI raw 25; AI casual/humanized 40; AI-polished human 15. At least one third priority job-search genres, with humans and AI. Include a prespecified 30–60-whitespace-word slice (inclusive); longer samples 61–500 words. Select without detector outcomes, retain all frozen examples regardless of scores. Human text may be a documented contiguous excerpt; never AI-correct it. Do not misrepresent historical correspondence or pedagogical assignments as authentic modern LinkedIn messages. Native/proficient proxy does not establish individual L1. Avoid all builder collections listed in brief/request and prior-round human collections/authors where feasible. Deduplicate exact and normalized text against rounds 1–2 and within this set. Model identifiers are requested proxy provenance, not authenticated model identities.

Thresholds: local detection >=0.50 (phase B only); ZeroGPT data.fakePercentage >=50; report local verify FAIL separately (phase B only). Never interpret an unavailable prediction as human. ZeroGPT starts at least 5.3 seconds after the previous request finishes, including retries; exponential/polite backoff and Retry-After honored. Save all raw HTTP response bodies, parsed answers and attempt timestamps. A persistent challenge/denial stops submissions; no bypass.

Fixed quality score for phase B, unchanged: 10 * (0.40 * human specificity + 0.20 * raw-AI sensitivity + 0.40 * casual/humanized-AI sensitivity). Polishing excluded from this score. Also report all-text and polishing-excluded accuracy. Parity requires at least ZeroGPT non-polished accuracy, no higher aggregate human FPR and no lower hard-AI sensitivity. Report uncertainty/source clustering, short slice, priority genres and bias subgroups. Prior-round local reruns belong to phase B; reuse their saved ZeroGPT outputs.

Phase A deliverables: frozen texts.json and manifest.json; zerogpt.json raw answers; PHASE_A.md and summary.json with composition, sources, hash and completion/category flag counts. Stop before local evaluation. No quality/parity/frontier verdict is issued in phase A.
