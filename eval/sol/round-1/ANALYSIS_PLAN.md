# Round 1 analysis plan

Written after freezing texts.json, before inspecting any full-run detector outcomes. Three API probes on H001 established connectivity and response shapes only. No text was changed after any probe.

- Fixed detector thresholds: ours ai_score >= 0.50; ZeroGPT fakePercentage >= 50. The /verify gate is reported independently as FAIL versus PASS, using default max_ai=0.30 and its tell rules. Errors/incomplete results are not treated as predictions.
- Five required strata: human native/proficient (30; individual native-language status unverified), documented human non-native (30), raw AI (20), casual/humanized/non-native-imitation AI (30), AI-polished human (10).
- Report overall accuracy both with all 60 AI-derived examples positive and with the 10 AI-polished human cases excluded. The latter avoids pretending polishing establishes wholly machine-authored provenance. Also report balanced accuracy, confusion counts, raw flagged rates, and the strict gate.
- Score out of 10: 10 * (0.40 * human specificity across all 60 humans + 0.20 * raw AI sensitivity + 0.40 * casual/humanized AI sensitivity). This puts most of the AI-detection weight on the requested hard cases; polishing is excluded from the score because the intended target label is debatable. Apply the identical scorecard to ZeroGPT as a reference. Round to one decimal. This is an explicitly constructed diagnostic score, not a calibrated universal quality rating.
- The yes/no line refers to this sample, not a population-wide equivalence claim. Say yes only if ours has at least ZeroGPT's non-polished overall accuracy, no higher aggregate human false-positive rate, and no lower casual/humanized sensitivity. Report all category tradeoffs even when that rule yields yes.
- Pairwise discordance and exact two-sided McNemar p-value on the non-polished subset; approximate Wilson intervals for category rates. These are descriptive because sources, prompts, and derivative pairs cluster. No tuning or sample replacement based on outcomes.
- Audit valid coverage, engine versions in verify responses, hashes, and endpoint/gate score disagreement. If service versions change, do not silently pool them as one model.
- Limitations to disclose: dataset source novelty is only relative to the builder's exclusion list, not proof of absence from detector pretraining; FCE exam tasks and historical business samples are domain proxies; unknown L1 in the native/proficient stratum; no authentic pre-2022 LinkedIn or modern application-form answers; short texts dominate; four AI model IDs are proxy-reported identifiers; related original/paraphrase/polish samples are not statistically independent.
